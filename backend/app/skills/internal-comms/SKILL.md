- First line: the one thing the reader must know or do, and by when.
- Status reports: Progress, Plans, Problems (with the help needed), each 2 to 4 bullets with numbers.
- Announcements: what changes, who is affected, when, what to do, who to ask.
- FAQs: real questions in the reader's words; answers of 1 to 3 sentences.
- Plain, warm, direct tone; no hype and no blame. Dates as "Mon 14 Mar", owners by name or role.
