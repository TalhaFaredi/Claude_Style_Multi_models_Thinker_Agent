- Start from the purpose and audience, then pick one clear visual direction and say it in one line.
- Palette: 1 main color, 1 accent, 2 to 3 neutrals. Give hex codes. Text contrast at least 4.5:1.
- Type: at most 2 families (one for headings, one for body). Give sizes as a scale (for example 34 / 21 / 14 px).
- Layout: a grid, generous white space, one focal point per screen, aligned edges.
- Hierarchy: size, weight and position first; color last. States must differ by more than color.
- Avoid the generic look: no purple gradients by default, no emoji as icons, no random drop shadows.
- Give the reason behind each choice in a few words, and one alternative direction if taste matters.
