name: humanizer
description: Make text read like a careful person wrote it, without changing its claims.
triggers: [humanize, humanizer, sound human, sounds like ai, less ai, natural tone, more natural, polish, rewrite, blog post, linkedin, cover letter, essay]
modes: [simple, thinker, planner]
source: based on https://github.com/blader/humanizer (MIT) and Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing"; shortened for this app
---
Keep every fact and claim. Change only how it is said.
Remove these patterns:
- Staging instead of stating: "It's not X, it's Y", one-line dramatic closers, "Here's the thing", answering objections nobody raised.
- Rhythm by rule: forced groups of three, every sentence starting the same way, dash after dash, stacked qualifiers.
- Inflation: "delve", "tapestry", "testament", "pivotal", "seamless", "robust", "in today's fast-paced world", vague "experts say".
- Formatting by rule: bold on random phrases, a heading over every paragraph, bullet lists for flowing ideas.
- Chat leftovers: "Great question", "I hope this helps", "As an AI", "Certainly!", summaries of what you just said.
Write plainly: concrete nouns, active verbs, varied sentence length, specific examples.
Say "is" and "has" instead of "serves as" and "boasts". Cut words that add nothing.
