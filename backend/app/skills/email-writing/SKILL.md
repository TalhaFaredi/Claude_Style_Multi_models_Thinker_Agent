- Subject line: specific, under 8 words.
- First sentence: why you are writing. Second: what you need, and by when.
- Keep it under 150 words unless the user asks for more; one topic per email.
- Make the ask easy to answer (a yes/no, two time options, a clear deadline).
- Match the relationship: warm for colleagues, formal for first contact, calm for complaints.
- Close with a short thank-you and the sender's name placeholder [Name].
Offer a shorter and a more formal variant only if the tone is unclear.
