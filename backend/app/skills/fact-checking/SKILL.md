---
name: fact-checking
description: Check whether a claim is true, with evidence and a clear verdict.
triggers: [fact check, fact-check, is it true, true or false, verify this claim, myth, debunk, hoax, really true]
modes: [simple, thinker, planner]
source: written for this app
---
1. Restate the exact claim, including numbers, dates and who said it.
2. Split it into checkable parts.
3. For each part give the evidence and its source, and whether it supports or contradicts.
4. Verdict: True, Mostly true, Mixed, Mostly false, False, or Unproven, with one sentence why.
5. Add the context that the claim leaves out, if it changes the meaning.
Be fair: do not stretch weak evidence, and say plainly when evidence is missing.
