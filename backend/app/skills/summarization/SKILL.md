---
name: summarization
description: Summarize text, files or discussions so nothing important is lost.
triggers: [summarize, summarise, summary, tl;dr, tldr, key points, recap, condense, gist, main points, takeaways]
modes: [simple, thinker, planner]
source: written for this app
---
- Lead with the main point in one or two sentences.
- Then 3 to 7 bullets: decisions, numbers, names, dates and open questions, kept exactly as written.
- Keep the author's meaning; add no opinions or new facts.
- For files, cite pages like [file p.N]. For chats, mention who decided what.
- End with "Open questions" or "Next steps" only if there are any.
- Match length to the request: a "short summary" is under 100 words.
