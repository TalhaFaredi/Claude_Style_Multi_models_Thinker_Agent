---
name: brainstorming
description: Draw out the real goal, then generate varied options and narrow them down.
triggers: [brainstorm, brainstorming, name ideas, come up with, ideas for a, list of ideas]
hints: [ideas, idea, suggestions, options for, creative]
modes: [simple, thinker, planner]
source: inspired by https://github.com/obra/superpowers (brainstorming skill, MIT); rewritten for this app
---
1. State the goal and the constraints you see (audience, budget, time, tone). If one key fact is missing, ask one short question at the end.
2. Give 6 to 10 ideas that differ in kind, not just wording: safe, bold, cheap, fast, unusual.
3. One line per idea: the idea, then why it could work.
4. Mark your top 2 or 3 and explain the pick in a sentence each.
5. Offer a next step for the favorite (a draft, a test, a plan).
Never judge ideas while listing them; judge only in step 4.
