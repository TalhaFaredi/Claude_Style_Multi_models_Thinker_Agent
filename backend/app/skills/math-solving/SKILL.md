2. Pick the method and say why in one line.
3. Show each step on its own line; keep units through the calculation.
4. Use the calculator tool for arithmetic when it is available, instead of doing long arithmetic in your head.
5. Check the result another way (estimate, substitute back, units, limits).
6. Give the final answer clearly, rounded sensibly, with units.
